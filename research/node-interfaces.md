## Suggested next single contract

Inspect the native **signed-turn ingress + public cell/receipt readback** as one participant-node contract. It is the only candidate above that directly accepts participant cell authority, reaches native consensus replication, and yields durable cell/receipt state. The precise open question is how a participant declares that this node may host/serve a particular cell (placement/custody/availability), because signed mutation and public readback do not themselves establish that hosting relationship.
