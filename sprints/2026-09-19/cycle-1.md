## Concrete target

The source-owned contract and detailed lane boundaries live in [Mini's cycle document](../../../minidregg/docs/CYCLE-2026-09-19.md). One signed, persistent multi-resource journey through the compiled `minidregg-host` and a usable Rust client is the integration target:

1. Birth task/object and typed-content resources with source-authored laws and owner authority.
2. Delegate a restricted right and obtain authorized, scoped observations.
3. Change task and content atomically, evaluating every participating resource's current law against the actual joint final state.
4. Refuse an invalid second leg without publishing the valid first leg.
5. Preserve grants across policy replacement; perform explicit authorized revocation; retain deliberate management lockout.
6. Restart and retry the original signed bytes without repeating accepted work or inventing a new receipt.
7. Refuse missing, excessive and wrong-kind observation authority through the public receiving path.

These are acceptance targets, not claims that the complete journey has passed. Existing historical fixture passes remain evidence at their original scope.
