observe delegation, verified historical issuance, current dispatch admission,
current launch claims, and a browser/API entrance using those results. Share

extract the selected article, and perform separate recipient admission before
acknowledging consumption. A local packet supplied to op20 does not test fn
receiving. Real-model hosting also needs provider request limits that bound
cost before sending; post-response settlement alone cannot prevent an external
over-budget response. These are active implementation obligations, not scope
reductions. Current ownership lives in the graph.
