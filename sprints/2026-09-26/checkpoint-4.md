## Physical interruption and recovery

Both Mac and Linux native grain recipes reproduced the same real defect: hard disconnect killed the child, but the controller sometimes observed the exit first and settled it as ordinary completion. The signed parent then had no held allowance, contradicting the expected interruption/reconciliation state. The failures are preserved; they were not accepted as green.

Mini [f195ba9](https://github.com/emberian/minidregg/commit/f195ba9) adds an atomic choice between worker completion and cancellation.
