## Acceptance and exclusions

Component tests are necessary evidence, not Gate A/B/C completion. Capture the
exact source and binary used for each native journey. A qualified older fn image
and a newer development image are different qualifications. Run new experiments
in fresh isolated Stores and transport nodes. Do not retry the preserved r3

The common path must expose current-law refusals and preserve historical exact
outcomes. Namespace reservation does not grant authority. Enrollment admits a
signing principal; it does not automatically grant resource rights. Application
routing must preserve signed request bytes rather than normalize them. Transport
ACK, Mini admission and external process completion remain distinct.

At batch end record what actually ran, source pins, remaining failure, and next
consumer. Do not close the broader platform goal merely because the timebox ends.
